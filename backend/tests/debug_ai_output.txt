# Ingliz Tilida O'zim Haqimda Gapirish: Ism, Millat, Kasb

## Kirish
Ingliz tilida o'z haqingizda gapirish (self-introduction) muloqotning eng asosiy qismlaridan biridir. Bu darsda siz ismingiz, millatingiz va kasbingiz haqida oddiy va aniq gapirishni o'rganasiz. Bu ma'lumotlar yangi odamlar bilan tanishishda, ish intervyularida yoki rasmiy vaziyatlarda juda foydali bo'ladi.

## Asosiy Tarkib

### 1. Ism Haqida Gapirish (Talking About Your Name)
Ingliz tilida ismingizni aytishning bir necha usullari mavjud:

**Oddiy ifodalar:**
- "My name is [Ism]." (Mening ismim [Ism].)
- "I am [Ism]." (Men [Ism].)
- "You can call me [Ism]." (Meni [Ism] deb chaqirishingiz mumkin.)

**Misol:**
- "My name is Aziza." (Mening ismim Aziza.)
- "I am Aziz." (Men Aziz.)
- "You can call me John." (Meni Jon deb chaqirishingiz mumkin.)

**Qo'shimcha ma'lumot:**
- "My full name is [To'liq ism]." (Mening to'liq ismim [To'liq ism].)
- "My first name is [Ism] and my last name is [Familiya]." (Mening ismim [Ism] va familiyam [Familiya].)

### 2. Millat Haqida Gapirish (Talking About Your Nationality)
Millatingiz haqida gapirishda quyidagi iboralardan foydalanishingiz mumkin:

**Asosiy ifodalar:**
- "I am from [Davlat]." (Men [Davlat]danman.)
- "I come from [Davlat]." (Men [Davlat]dan kelganman.)
- "I am [Millat]." (Men [Millat]man.)

**Misol:**
- "I am from Uzbekistan." (Men O'zbekistondanman.)
- "I come from Tashkent, Uzbekistan." (Men Toshkent, O'zbekistondan kelganman.)
- "I am Uzbek." (Men o'zbekman.)

**Davlat va millat nomlari:**
- Uzbekistan - Uzbek (O'zbekiston - o'zbek)
- USA - American (AQSH - amerikalik)
- Russia - Russian (Rossiya - rus)
- Turkey - Turkish (Turkiya - turk)
- China - Chinese (Xitoy - xitoylik)

### 3. Kasb Haqida Gapirish (Talking About Your Profession)
Kasbingiz haqida gapirishning turli usullari:

**Oddiy ifodalar:**
- "I am a [Kasb]." (Men [Kasb]man.)
- "I work as a [Kasb]." (Men [Kasb] sifatida ishlayman.)
- "My profession is [Kasb]." (Mening kasbim [Kasb].)

**Misol:**
- "I am a teacher." (Men o'qituvchiman.)
- "I work as a doctor." (Men shifokor sifatida ishlayman.)
- "My profession is engineer." (Mening kasbim muhandis.)

**Mashhur kasblar:**
- Student (Talaba)
- Teacher (O'qituvchi)
- Doctor (Shifokor)
- Engineer (Muhandis)
- Businessman (Biznesmen)
- Driver (Haydovchi)
- Programmer (Dasturchi)

### 4. Barchasini Birlashtirish (Putting It All Together)
Keling, barcha ma'lumotlarni bitta gapda birlashtiramiz:

**Namuna:**
"Hello! My name is Alisher. I am from Uzbekistan. I am Uzbek. I am a student at Tashkent University."

Yoki:
"Let me introduce myself. I'm Malika. I come from Samarkand, Uzbekistan. I work as a teacher at school number 15."

## Grammatika Eslatmalari

1. **"a" artikli**: Kasb haqida gapirganda "a" artiklidan foydalaning:
   - To'g'ri: "I am a teacher." (Men o'qituvchiman.)
   - Noto'g'ri: "I am teacher."

2. **Katta harf**: Millat va davlat nomlari har doim katta harf bilan yoziladi:
   - To'g'ri: "I am Uzbek." "I am from Uzbekistan."
   - Noto'g'ri: "I am uzbek." "I am from uzbekistan."

3. **So'z tartibi**: Oddiy gapda tartib: Subject + Verb + Object
   - "I + am + a teacher."

## O'zaro Suhbat (Dialogue Practice)

**Namuna suhbat:**
A: Hello! What's your name?
B: Hi! My name is Farida. And you?
A: I'm Tom. Where are you from, Farida?
B: I'm from Uzbekistan. I'm Uzbek.
A: That's interesting! What do you do?
B: I'm a doctor. I work at a hospital in Tashkent.

## Vazifalar (Assignments)

### Vazifa 1: To'ldirish
Quyidagi gaplarni to'g'ri so'zlar bilan to'ldiring:

1. My ______ is Dilshod.
2. I am ______ Uzbekistan.
3. I ______ a student.
4. My profession ______ engineer.
5. I come ______ Tashkent.

### Vazifa 2: O'zingiz haqingizda gapiring
Quyidagi savollarga javob bering:

1. What is your name?
2. Where are you from?
3. What is your nationality?
4. What is your profession?
5. Where do you work/study?

### Vazifa 3: Tarjima
Quyidagi gaplarni ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling:

1. Mening ismim Sarvar.
2. Men O'zbekistondanman.
3. Men o'zbekman.
4. Men dasturchiman.
5. Men Toshkentda yashayman.

### Vazifa 4: Suhbat yozish
O'zingiz va do'stingiz o'rtasida qisqa suhbat yozing. Suhbatda quyidagi ma'lumotlar bo'lsin:
- Ismlar
- Millat
- Kasblar
- Yashash joylari

### Vazifa 5: To'g'ri/Noto'g'ri
Quyidagi gaplarning to'g'ri yoki noto'g'ri ekanligini aniqlang va noto'g'ri bo'lsa, to'g'rilashtiring:

1. "I am doctor."
2. "I am from uzbekistan."
3. "My name is a Alisher."
4. "I am Uzbek."
5. "I work teacher."

## Qo'shimcha Manbalar

1. **Foydali iboralar:**
   - "Nice to meet you!" (Tanishganimdan xursandman!)
   - "How about you?" (Sizchi?)
   - "What about you?" (Siz haqingizda?)

2. **Murakkabroq ifodalar:**
   - "I have been working as a [kasb] for [yil] years." (Men [yil] yildan beri [kasb] sifatida ishlayman.)
   - "I was born in [shahar]." (Men [shahar]da tug'ilganman.)

3. **Tavsiyalar:**
   - Har kuni o'zingiz haqingizda gapirishni mashq qiling
   - O'z ovozingizni yozib oling va tinglang
   - Ingliz tilidagi filmlar va seriallarni tinglang
